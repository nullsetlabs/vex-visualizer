What the words on this page mean

The season

Override
The VEX V5 Robotics Competition game for the 2026-2027 season. Two alliances score Pins and Cups on Goals. See the Override game tab.
Event
One competition (a tournament). Most last one or two days.
League
An event that runs over several weeks, with matches on different days.
Signature event
The top tier of events in the season. Large, strong fields, and awards there can qualify teams for the World Championship.
Worlds
The VEX Robotics World Championship at the end of the season. A Worlds qualifier is a team that has earned a spot.
HS, MS, HS + MS
High school, middle school, and events where both compete together. Blue marks high school, purple marks middle school, teal marks both.

Matches and results

Alliance
Two teams that play together in a match, red or blue.
Qualification matches
The regular matches every team plays. They decide the rankings.
Elimination matches
The playoff rounds after qualifications: round of 16, quarterfinals, semifinals and finals.
Record
Wins, losses and ties in qualification matches, written as W-L-T.
Tournament Champions
The alliance that wins the finals. Shown with a trophy.
Excellence Award
The top award at an event, for a team that is strong all round. Shown with a star.
Design Award
For an excellent engineering design process and notebook. Shown with a pen.

Skills

Robot skills
A one-minute solo run. Driver skills are driven by a student; autonomous skills run on the robot's own program. The skills score adds the best of each.
Skills standings
Every team's best skills score this season, ranked worldwide by VEX.

Numbers in the rankings

Win points
Official ranking points from winning (or tying) qualification matches.
Auton points
Official points from the autonomous part of matches, used to break ties in the rankings.
Schedule points
Official tie-breaker based on how strong a team's opponents were.
OPR
Offensive power rating: an estimate of how many points a team adds to its alliance's score. Computed by this site from qualification match scores, so it is a guide, not an official number.
CCWM
An estimate of how much a team adds to its alliance's winning margin. Also computed by this site.

How often the data updates

Signature events
Every 30 minutes while the event is running, so rankings, matches and awards appear during the event.
All other events
Twice a week, on Monday and Thursday mornings (UTC). A tournament usually appears here within three or four days.
Skills standings
With every update; at most every 3 hours during signature events.
